# Energy Concentrated Wavelet Transform for Transient Signal Time-Frequency Analysis [with MATLAB Code]

In practical applications, some signals captured are composed of a series of patterns with multi-valued time-varying instantaneous frequency (IF), namely transient signals, which makes some advanced time-frequency analysis (TFA) tools ineffective or even invalid. Fortunately, the group delay (GD) of such signals can be well represented as a single-valued function of frequency. Considering that the frequency domain signal model is suitable for describing GD, we developed a post-processing technique called Wavelet Transformation (WT) Time Redistribution Synchronized Compression Transform (WTSST). In WTSST, the spread TF coefficients in the wavelet transform are aggregated into the GD of the transient signal, aiming to generate a more concentrated time-frequency representation (TFR), and WTSST allows the recovery of all or part of the signal. Considering the analytical wavelet transform under Gaussian functions, a theoretical analysis of the spatial correlation of the wavelet transform was given, including an analysis of the candidate precision of GD and the reconstruction precision of the signal. Based on the theoretical analysis of WTSST, by introducing the fixed point iteration method, a Time Redistribution Multi-synchronized Compression Transform based on WTSST (WTMSST) was proposed, further improving the shortcomings of WTSST that cannot accurately describe strong frequency-varying signals. Simulations and real signal analysis show that this method can well describe the characteristics of transient signals.
